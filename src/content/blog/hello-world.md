This is the first note in a new personal space for writing about AI workflows, frontend platforms, and product craft.

I want this blog to stay close to the work: small observations, practical patterns, and longer essays when a topic deserves more room. The center of gravity is the interface between people and intelligent systems.

## What I will write about

- AI workflows and the details that make them useful in real engineering teams.
- Frontend architecture that stays useful under real product pressure.
- Developer workflows with AI as a collaborator rather than a shortcut.
- Design decisions that make complex tools feel calm.

The goal is simple: write in public, sharpen ideas, and leave a useful trail.
